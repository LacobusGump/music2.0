The metronome, the tools and all thirty-three songs are free.
They stay free. If they were worth something to you, you can help back.

beGump is one person on a Mac Mini. No investors, no grants, no institution behind it. Everything here was built to be given away and then actually given away — the metronome with its rudiment trainer, the Wand, the focus tool, the Turbo language, the whole album streaming end to end, and a research page listing seventy-eight ideas that turned out to be wrong.

Free costs something to keep free. Domain, hosting, the machine it all runs on, and the hours. If you use any of it, this is where you can put something back.
