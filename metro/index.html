Cross-rhythm — evenly across the bar

Each layer lays k hits across n steps of the bar, spaced as evenly as the maths allows — a Euclidean rhythm, which is what nearly every traditional groove turns out to be. On the circle each layer is a ring, and its hits draw a shape. Drag a ring to turn it — the shape doesn't change, where it lands does. A snare at k2/n8 sits on 1 and 3; turned by 2 it's the backbeat. Tap a dot to accent it.

Two ways to work against the click. Bounce trades two figures — subdivisions or rudiments — every few bars, so you practise the switch itself; the circle flips faces each trade. Ladder climbs the pulse from quarters up to 64ths and holds each rung. Only one runs at a time. For getting faster or slower, use Ramp next to Tap.
